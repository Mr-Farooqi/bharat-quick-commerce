Frequently Asked Questions

How does BharatBasket achieve delivery in 10-15 minutes?

We partner with dedicated micro-fulfillment dark stores placed every 2-3 kilometers in residential zones. When you tap order, automated pick-lists allow items to be bagged in under 3 minutes, leaving our nearby fleet partner ample time to safely ride to your door.

What are the available payment methods?

We support all major payment instruments: Cash on Delivery (COD), UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), and Net Banking.

Is there any minimum order limit for free delivery?

Orders above ₹199 qualify for 100% Free Superfast Delivery! For smaller orders below ₹199, a nominal dispatch fee of ₹15-₹25 applies to support our delivery partner fleet.

Can I track my delivery in real-time?

Yes! Our customer application features a live WebSocket order stepper showing exact milestone transitions (Order Placed → Store Packing → Out for Delivery → Delivered) with direct 1-tap call access to the assigned rider.
